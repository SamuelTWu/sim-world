Proposal for a new video game:
Simulate a continent. Make biomes, then terrain. Then, introduce 100 ‘entities’, where an entity is an object with motivation. It has attributes like movement speed, health, abilities, etc. Simulate the world for N steps, then drop the player into the world. The goal is to make the world feel alive, and to make the player feel like they are just a small part of a larger world. 
How would structures work? All entities have a structure preference. Some may have no structure preference at all. Structure preference is determined by procedural modeling via graph grammars. By making them motivation and not set in stone, there are opportunities for interaction. Like-minded (need to define) entities could cooperate. Adversarial entities could ‘get in each others way’. 

The simulation should use a data-driven architecture in which Entity primarily owns attribute/state data while independent systems implement rules, dependencies, relationships, classification, and decision-making. Attributes such as patience, anger, courage, stress, and aggression should be represented as independent base, derived, temporary, environmental, or historical values, with dependencies defined through configuration or an attribute graph rather than hard-coded references between attribute classes (e.g., anger = f(patience, stress, provocation)). Separate systems such as AttributeSystem, RelationshipSystem, NeedSystem, MemorySystem, DecisionSystem, and IdentitySystem operate on this data. Entity identity should be derived bottom-up from attribute vectors rather than assigned through rigid species/archetype/role classes: archetypes define statistical distributions over attributes, and entities are classified using normalized weighted similarity or probabilistic distance, producing multiple concurrent classifications (e.g., Wolf=0.87, Hunter=0.71, Protector=0.63) across independent dimensions such as species, archetype, personality, role, profession, social identity, and behavioral pattern. Because classifications are derived from mutable attributes and experiences, identities can change dynamically and support hybrid or contradictory states without special-case code. The system should also distinguish objective classification, self-concept, and observer-specific social classification, allowing different entities or societies to maintain different beliefs about the same entity based on experience, reputation, culture, relationships, and misinformation. The resulting architecture forms a feedback loop in which attributes drive behavior and experiences, experiences influence classification and self-concept, and those identities subsequently influence future decisions and behavior; consequently, categories remain descriptive emergent properties of the simulation rather than control structures imposed by the developer.

Notes on graph grammers: 
grammers must be predifined: village architecture, city architecture, forrest, haunted, etc. These predefined grammers must also have defined constraints (blocks are given attribute tags, some of which must be preserved) in order to blend grammers, we need to make use of these constraints. 

So we first build the world. Then we spawn entities (those ARE our civilizations). They are given attributes, including a GRAPH GRAMMER. Based on those, they are given a species. They are then given building blocks based on their biome and species. They then have free reign to build as theyd like, but they may only do one action per turn, same as all other entities. It then becomes a simulation of entities either building, moving, fighting, trading, etc. The player can pause at any time and join the game. 

Every time an entity wants to build in a given area, it checks if another entity wants to build in that same area. If no, build. if yes, check if they are cooperating or nah. if they cooperate, blend the two styles together and continue building. 
if they do not cooperate, make them fight!

When a player STOP GENERATION, place bots in the biome, change entities to be more NPC, and play! (entities out of the players radius can continue to simulate?)
